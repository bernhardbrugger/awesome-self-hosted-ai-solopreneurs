**Show HN: Awesome Self-Hosted AI Stack for Solopreneurs (GDPR/EU-hosting lens)**

Most "self-hosted AI" lists are tech round-ups: what exists, not what happens
to your data. I built this one backwards — every entry answers "can I deploy
this on a normal Hetzner/OVH VPS" *and* "does it quietly phone home to a US
cloud." Tools that fail the second question stay listed (honesty over
completeness) but get a ⚠️ explaining exactly why: n8n's Sustainable Use
License, Ollama's no-auth-by-default binding, Hugging Face TGI's license
flip, Langfuse's gated enterprise modules.

There's also a compliance section citing actual regulator guidance (BfDI's
Dec 2025 "Handreichung KI") instead of generic "GDPR-compliant" marketing
claims, plus a ready-to-deploy Docker Compose quick-start (Ollama + Open WebUI +
Qdrant) to get a first EU-hosted stack running today.

Feedback and PRs welcome, especially on tools I haven't verified yet.
